# Describe what is, not what was left out

No "deliberately not included", no "this alternative was rejected", no marker written to stop a
future session re-opening a decision. The intent is to prevent re-litigation and the effect is
the opposite: it makes an absence into a documented entity that keeps arguing for itself. A
decision that was made leaves its result and nothing else.

This holds wherever prose is written — source comments, test headers, config templates that
ship to consumers, rules, skills, PRDs. Commit messages and PR bodies are outside it:
describing what changed is not marking an absence.

## Say what you mean, not a figure for it

No mannered prose. A metaphor substituted for a direct statement ("a dial worth turning" for "a
parameter worth varying", "earns its keep" for "still matters") makes the reader work to recover
the literal claim and drags in connotations the writer did not choose. When a literal phrase is
available, use it. The glossary's own terms (`witness valve`, `umbrella`, `surface`, `covenant`)
are vocabulary, not figures — they stay; what goes is the ornament around them.
